// 01 — About

Field notes

I'm a second-year B.Tech CSE student who'd rather build something than just read about how it works. Most of my questions come down to one thing: how do I actually ship this? That's taken me across web development (HTML/CSS/JS → React), backend work in Node and Flask, cloud deployment on GCP, and applied AI/ML — with cybersecurity as the thread that ties most of it together.

My work leans toward systems that watch, detect, or coordinate — fraud engines, IoT intelligence layers, campus logistics — the kind of projects where the interesting part is what happens when data starts moving in real time. Alongside that, I'm building a structured path into offensive security — TryHackMe, Hack The Box, PortSwigger labs — with an eye on OSCP down the line.

I learn best by going concept → simple explanation → example → code → execution, then repeating that loop for the next thing. And I'm increasingly focused on turning that into something concrete: real applications, real clients, work that holds up outside a classroom — not just another finished tutorial.
